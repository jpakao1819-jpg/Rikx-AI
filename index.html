HOW RIKX SHOULD LEARN

Principles over promises

01

Prefer credible sources

Use primary and established educational material. Treat marketing claims, influencer opinions, and profit screenshots as unverified—not evidence.

02

Measure honestly

Test chronologically on unseen data, include spreads and slippage, and compare with simple baselines. A high win rate alone proves little.

03

Protect the learner

Practice with simulated funds, communicate uncertainty, and use risk limits. This program does not connect to a broker or place trades.
